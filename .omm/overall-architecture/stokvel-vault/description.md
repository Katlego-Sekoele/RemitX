Solidity contract contracts/src/StokvelVault.sol (Hardhat project in contracts/). One deployment holds every stokvel (ADR 0002): `createStokvel`, `startCycle(members, roundStartTimes, deadlines, payoutTimes)`, `contribute(id, round, memberId)`, `finalise(id, round)`, admin `pause`/`unpause`, plus views (`getCycle`, `hasPaid`, `isFinalisable`, `openRound`...). Rounds fill in order; round N releases its pool to the Treasury Wallet when N and N+1 are fully paid and N's payout time has passed (D5/D12). Trusts the backend (Treasury Wallet is the only caller) to attribute contributions. ABI exported to contracts/abi/StokvelVault.json; deployment record in contracts/deployments/.
