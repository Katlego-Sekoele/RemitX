quote_service.py `price_remittance` is shared by `create_quote` (beneficiary-bound, persisted, time-boxed Quote; checks balance and limits early) and `preview_quote` (stateless rate browsing). Fee = fixed + percentage of sender amount (pinned to ZAR, converted to sender currency); margin applied to the rate; value locked as `Quote.sender_amount_zar`. exchange_rate_service.get_active_rate fetches lazily via exchange_rate_provider and caches in `exchange_rates`, tolerating staleness up to `MAX_RATE_STALENESS_HOURS`. send_limits.py computes daily/monthly usage from the ledger (SAST day/month) against tier × risk allowance; `require_can_send` is applied at quote and again at confirm.
