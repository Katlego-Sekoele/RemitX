kyc_onboarding.py walks the data-driven wizard catalogue (steps, requirements, editable statuses) to compute the next step, normalise PATCHes and list what submit still needs; country/jurisdiction/identity-scheme support comes from rows (`JurisdictionCatalogue`). identity_validators.py and sa_id_number.py validate identification numbers per scheme. kyc_risk_rules.py scores an application 0–100 at submission from signals (pep_declared 60, expected volume above tier limit, source of funds other, nationality ≠ residence, non-national ID doc — 25 each), bands it into a risk rating, and derives tier × risk allowances.
