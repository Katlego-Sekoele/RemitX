remittance_service.confirm_remittance: locks the sender row, guarded-flips the Quote ACTIVE→USED, re-runs `require_can_send` and balance checks, writes seven `pending` ledger legs sharing the quote_id (fee, net remittance to platform fiat, treasury→sender token, sender→beneficiary token, beneficiary→treasury token, token_burn treasury→issuer, beneficiary_payout platform fiat→beneficiary fiat) plus the Remittance row, commits, then enqueues `settle_remittance`. deposit_service.process_deposits reconciles an uploaded bank-statement CSV (fingerprint dedupe, reference → account match, auto-confirm or leave pending); `approve_pending_deposit` lets staff link unmatched lines. withdrawal_service settles synchronously to a verified bank account, deducting `CASH_OUT_FEE_RATE` into revenue. bank_account_service runs the add/verify/reject queue.
