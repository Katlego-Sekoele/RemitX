How value moves through the RemitX double-entry ledger: fiat enters via reconciled bank-statement lines, is priced into a quote using live FX rates, and on confirmation is split into seven legs — fee/margin to revenue, net amount to the platform's bank account, a UCTUSD pass-through via the beneficiary's token account, a burn back to the issuer, and the payout from the platform's bank account in the receiver currency to the beneficiary's fiat account. Withdrawals move fiat out to verified bank accounts. KYC evidence sets how much may flow.
