Supported fiat: ZAR, USD, NAD, ZWL (PAYOUT_CURRENCIES); the token is UCTUSD (XRPL issued `uctusd`, moving to an ERC-20 on the XRPL EVM Testnet). Ledger rows use `credit_account_id` as source and `debit_account_id` as destination. In the stokvel extension a Stokvel gets its own token-currency account (type STOKVEL, decision D9) and becomes the sender of its round's payout remittance.
