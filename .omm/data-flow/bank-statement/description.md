Simulated daily reconciliation. Staff build a CSV on the admin Statement CSV page (frontend lib/bank-statement-csv.ts; sample at api/scripts/sample_bank_statement.csv) and submit it on Process Deposits; `deposit_service.process_deposits` parses each line (date, positive amount, currency, reference), fingerprints it for dedupe and credits the matching customer account or leaves it pending for manual approval. Customers find their deposit reference on the Accounts "Add money" panel.
