The seven `transactions` rows of one remittance share `quote_id` and move together: `pending` (written by confirm) → `processing` (claimed by burn_treasury_tokens) → `confirmed` (confirm_treasury_burn, balances applied) or `failed` (burn error, no balance change). Leg types: fee, remittance ×4 (net to platform fiat; treasury→sender token; sender→beneficiary token; beneficiary→treasury token), token_burn, beneficiary_payout. Note the column convention: `credit_account_id` is the source, `debit_account_id` the destination.
