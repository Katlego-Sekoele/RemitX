`quotes.status`: ACTIVE → USED via the guarded flip in `QuoteRepository.mark_used` (WHERE status='ACTIVE' AND expires_at > now) inside `confirm_remittance`. EXPIRED exists in the vocabulary but nothing sweeps quotes to it: an expired quote normally stays ACTIVE with a past `expires_at`, and the guard refuses it. A quote locks amounts, fee, margin, rates, receiver amount and the rand value used for limit accounting. If the post-flip limit or balance re-check fails, the transaction rolls back and the quote stays ACTIVE.
